# PUL7SAR BRAND GUIDELINES

Version: 1.0

This document defines the official visual identity and branding rules for PUL7SAR.

All future designs, templates, graphics, UI elements, animations, and marketing materials must follow these guidelines.

---

# Brand Vision

PUL7SAR is a premium global sports media brand.

The brand should immediately communicate:

- Professionalism
- Speed
- Trust
- Modern Sports Culture
- Premium Quality

The identity must never feel generic or outdated.

---

# Brand Personality

PUL7SAR should always feel:

- Premium
- Confident
- Athletic
- Modern
- Dynamic
- Minimal
- Intelligent

Avoid:

- Clickbait
- Visual clutter
- Cheap effects
- Excessive decorations

---

# Logo

The official logo is PUL7SAR.

The logo must remain visually consistent across every platform.

The logo should always appear in a clean and readable area.

Never stretch the logo.

Never rotate the logo.

Never apply random visual effects.

---

# Color Palette

## Primary Accent

Electric Blue

HEX:

#0070FF

Purpose:

Primary brand accent.

Lighting.

Highlights.

Interactive elements.

---

## Secondary Accent

PUL7SAR Red

HEX:

#E10600

Purpose:

Breaking News

Alerts

Urgent situations

Special campaigns

The red color should never replace the blue as the primary identity.

---

## Background

Primary Background:

Dark Charcoal

Secondary:

Dark Stadium Texture

Backgrounds should feel cinematic.

---

## Typography

Main text:

Premium White

Secondary text:

Metallic Silver

Accent text:

Electric Blue

Breaking text:

PUL7SAR Red

Typography must always maintain strong readability.

---

# Visual Style

Every design should communicate movement.

Preferred visual elements include:

- Stadium lighting
- Atmospheric particles
- Turf textures
- Motion blur
- Dynamic shadows
- Premium gradients

Avoid excessive visual noise.

---

# Image Style

Preferred:

Professional player photos

Official club assets

Premium typography

Minimal overlays

Fallback:

If no player photo exists, use DNA Mode with typography-driven layouts.

---

# Card Design Principles

Every card should include:

- Strong headline
- Balanced spacing
- High contrast
- Consistent margins
- Brand logo
- Professional footer

Cards should remain readable even on small mobile screens.

---

# Visual Hierarchy

Priority order:

1. Headline

2. Main Subject

3. Supporting Information

4. Branding

5. Footer

Nothing should compete with the headline.

---

# Animation Philosophy

Future animations should be:

Fast

Smooth

Elegant

Purposeful

Avoid distracting motion.

---

# Editorial Design

Headlines should be:

Short

Powerful

Professional

Never sensational.

Never misleading.

---

# Social Media Identity

Every platform should immediately feel like PUL7SAR.

Templates may adapt dimensions, but never change the identity.

---

# Future Expansion

The identity should support:

Website

Mobile Apps

Video Graphics

Motion Graphics

Live Match Center

Interactive Statistics

AI Generated Graphics

Without requiring a redesign.

---

# Brand Rule

If a design looks like it could belong to another sports page,

it is not a PUL7SAR design.

Every design must be instantly recognizable as PUL7SAR.

---

End of Version 1.0
